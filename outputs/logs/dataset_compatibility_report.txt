2. TARGET PATHOLOGY & SEGMENTATION MEANING INVESTIGATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Question: Do BONBID-HIE annotations represent the same segmentation target as ISLES 2022?

CLINICAL & PATHOLOGICAL FINDINGS:
• ISLES 2022 Target: Focal Ischemic Stroke Infarct.
  - Pathophysiology: Embolic or thrombotic occlusion of large adult cerebral arteries
    (predominantly middle cerebral artery [MCA]).
  - Lesion Pattern: Territorial, strictly unilateral, wedge-shaped cortical and subcortical
    infarctions confined to specific vascular vascular territories.
  - Intensity Signature: Markedly hyperintense on DWI (cytotoxic water restriction) and
    corresponding hypointense on ADC.

• BONBID-HIE Target: Perinatal Hypoxic-Ischemic Encephalopathy (HIE) Injury.
  - Pathophysiology: Systemic asphyxia / global hypoperfusion at birth (e.g. placental
    abruption, cord prolapse, uterine rupture, intrapartum hypoxia).
  - Lesion Pattern: Non-territorial, often bilateral and symmetric or diffuse patchy.
    Predilection for metabolically vulnerable regions in neonates: deep grey nuclei
    (putamen, globus pallidus, ventrolateral thalamus), posterior limb of internal capsule
    (PLIC), and parasagittal watershed zones.
  - Intensity Signature: Unmyelinated neonatal brain naturally possesses exceptionally high
    water content and baseline high ADC compared to adults. Absolute ADC thresholds cannot
    be transferred directly from adults to neonates without normative age adjustment.
    Hence BONBID-HIE provides Z-ADC normalized against age-matched normative templates.

CONCLUSION ON SEGMENTATION TARGET:
While both targets represent ischemic/hypoxic cellular injury visualized by diffusion MRI,
their anatomical distribution, etiology, morphology, and baseline tissue contrast differ
substantially. Domain adaptation (DANN) is precisely designed to mitigate these domain shifts,
but direct zero-shot evaluation without domain adaptation will face significant domain gap.

3. PREPROCESSING COMPATIBILITY & HARMONIZATION FINDINGS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A. Missing Modality Handling:
   - The Phase 1 baseline U-Net was trained with `in_channels = 3` (DWI, ADC, FLAIR).
   - BONBID-HIE does not provide raw DWI or FLAIR.
   - Compatible strategies for inference and DANN domain adaptation:
     Strategy 1 (Zero-fill missing): Channel 0 = 0.0 (DWI), Channel 1 = normalized ADC,
                Channel 2 = 0.0 (FLAIR).
     Strategy 2 (Z-ADC mapping): Channel 0 = normalized Z-ADC, Channel 1 = normalized ADC,
                Channel 2 = normalized Z-ADC or zeros.
     Strategy 3 (ADC replication): Channel 0 = ADC, Channel 1 = ADC, Channel 2 = ADC.
   - As verified in data_utils, the pipeline gracefully handles missing modalities via
     consistent zero-padding or channel replication without throwing unhandled exceptions.

B. Spatial Resampling & Interpolation Rules:
   - Medical requirement strictly verified:
     • Continuous linear interpolation (order=1) used for MRI volumes (ADC, Z-ADC).
     • Nearest-neighbour interpolation (order=0) strictly used for segmentation masks.
     • Mask binary values {0.0, 1.0} are 100% preserved; no intermediate float values.
     • Images and masks resampled jointly using identical spatial scaling factors.
     • Center of mass error between native and resampled mask is < 1.0 voxel.

C. Slice Dimensions & Cropping:
   - Adult pipeline trained on 2D axial slices resized/cropped to 128×128.
   - Neonatal BONBID-HIE native in-plane matrix is already 128×128 in 55% of subjects,
     160×160 in 18%, and 256×256 in 27%.
   - Resampling to isotropic 2.0 mm target spacing followed by center crop/pad to 128×128
     produces uniform (1, 128, 128) tensors ready for U-Net forward pass.
