── 4. CLINICAL & DOMAIN GAP INTERPRETATION ─────────────────────────────────────────
1. Domain Shift Impact: The adult baseline model achieved a mean Dice of 0.1451 on the
   neonatal cohort without adaptation, down from 0.2673 on the adult ISLES validation split.
2. Lesion Burden Sensitivity: For large, severe neonatal HIE infarcts (e.g. MGHNICU_002,
   24,480 voxels), zero-shot performance reached Dice = 0.4320 (IoU = 0.2755). However, for
   smaller, focal injuries (MGHNICU_001, 342 voxels), Dice was limited to 0.0480.
3. Missing Modality Gap: Adult stroke features are heavily anchored on DWI hyperintensity.
   Without raw DWI, directly zero-padding DWI extinguishes model activations (Dice = 0.0000),
   whereas feeding Z-ADC across auxiliary channels preserves diffusion feature flow.
4. Readiness for Domain Adaptation: This zero-shot evaluation establishes the exact
   quantitative lower-bound benchmark for Phase 4 (DANN domain adaptation).
